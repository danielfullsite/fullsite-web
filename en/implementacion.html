Process

Six steps. No ambiguous stages.

Every step has a defined outcome. The implementation does not move forward until the previous step is validated.

01
Assessment

We review the operation, network, terminals, printers, current system, menu, users and the restaurant’s needs. Outcome: an implementation roadmap with a defined timeline.

📋Review of existing equipment — POS, tablets, printers
🌐Network and connectivity analysis
📊Review of menu, users and current workflows
📅Implementation timeline
02
Configuration

We set up locations, terminals, stations, users, permissions and operating workflows tailored to the restaurant’s specific operation.

🏪Locations and terminals configured
👤Users, roles and permissions
🖨Printers and kitchen routing
⚙️Service and cashier workflows
03
Migration

We import and review the menu, products, recipes, inventory and any data available from the previous system. Quality and coverage are validated before migrating.

🍽Menu, products and modifiers
📦Opening inventory and storage areas
🔬Recipes and ingredients with quantities
✅Data quality validation
04
Simulation

We test orders, kitchen, printing, cashier, end-of-day closes, offline mode, recovery and sync before switching systems. The team practices in a real environment.

🧪Full flow from order to close
📴Offline operation test
🔄Sync and recovery
👥Training for the entire team
05
Supervised shift

Fullsite goes live during a supervised window, with a backup and a documented recovery procedure. Real-time support.

🟢Go-live with the support team on site
🛡Previous system available as backup
📡Real-time monitoring
📞Direct channel to the Fullsite team
06
Stabilization

We monitor the operation, resolve incidents and train each user for their role. The system is validated for day-to-day operation.

📈Monitoring of the first shifts
🔧Fine-tuning of the configuration
🎓Training by role and function
✅Autonomous operation sign-off

Real-time example
Chicken cost rose 18% this week — margin impact
What happenedThe supplier updated the price of chicken breast from $157/kg to $185/kg. Detected when logging Monday’s purchase.
SourcePurchasing module — inventory receiving log
Why it mattersChicken is the main ingredient in 3 top dishes. The Chicken Bowl’s food cost goes from 25.3% to 29.8%.
ImpactEstimated margin loss: ~$4,200 MXN/week if the price is not adjusted.
ActionAdjust the Chicken Bowl price from $185 to $215 MXN to keep the 72% target margin.
AlternativeRenegotiate with the supplier or switch to a temporary supplier.
Authorized byShift manager — 2:47 p.m. — from the manager panel
Status✓ Approved — applying menu change
Action takenChicken Bowl price updated to $215 MXN across all terminals and digital menus.
RecordChange documented with time, user and reason in the price history.
Result3 days later: Chicken Bowl food cost at 25.1%. Margin recovered.
ImpactSales of the dish dropped 8%, but total margin for the week rose +2.1 points.
Pattern detectedThis supplier has adjusted prices 3 times in the last 6 months. Fullsite records it to trigger earlier alerts in the future.
What you get

You don’t need more charts. You need to know what requires attention.

Close-of-day prediction
Estimates how the day might end before closing time so you can react while it still matters.
Cancellations and exceptions
Flags cancellations, discounts, comps and movements outside the restaurant’s normal behavior.
Theoretical vs. actual inventory
Every sale deducts stock per the recipe. Spots differences between what should exist and what actually exists.
Team performance
Sales, average ticket, activity and results by user, shift and location — in real time.
Profitability per product
Cross-references popularity, price and cost to identify products worth reviewing, promoting or adjusting.
Cost variations
Detects changes in ingredients, suppliers and recipes that could be eating into your margin.
