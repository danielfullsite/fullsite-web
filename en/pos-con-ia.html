AI-powered POS

AI POS for restaurants: the point of sale that tells you what to do

A regular POS records what happened. An AI POS tells you what shouldn't have happened, what it cost and what to check tomorrow.

Chat on WhatsApp →Book a demo

In short: an AI POS charges, sends orders to the kitchen and tracks inventory like any point of sale, but it also compares every day against your restaurant's history and alerts you to cash leaks, end-of-day differences and sales changes, with the amount at stake.

What an AI POS does that a traditional one doesn't

  • Catches leaks: voids after payment, unusual discounts and comps.
  • Reconciles the end-of-day report and shows where the difference is.
  • Explains sales: why you dropped vs. last week, which dish, which shift.
  • Controls food cost by recipe.
  • Answers questions with the exact number.
  • Alerts you on your phone.

Example alerts (sample amounts)

  • “7 voids after payment tonight, 3x the usual. Amount: $2,140.”
  • “Yesterday's close is $860 short on card payments. Check tickets 1182 to 1190.”
  • “Your best seller's cost rose 9% this week. Check the recipe or the price.”
