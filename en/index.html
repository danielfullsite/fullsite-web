Frequently asked questions

How long does installation take?
It depends on the size of your operation: terminals, printers and integrations. After the initial assessment we define the path, and your current POS keeps running during the transition.
Do I need to buy special hardware?
No. Fullsite runs in the browser, on compatible computers and tablets. No local servers, no proprietary terminals. The goal is to make the most of the hardware you already have.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. Every terminal keeps taking orders, sending them to the kitchen and processing payments even if the connection goes down. When it comes back, everything syncs automatically.
Can you migrate my data from another POS?
Yes. We've already migrated an entire restaurant from another POS: over 500 menu items, 178 recipes with ingredients, 200 suppliers and nearly three years of sales history.
Do I need to be tech-savvy?
No. Alerts and recommendations arrive on your phone via WhatsApp. No dashboards to open, no new software to learn.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. It's month to month. If you're not seeing value, cancel whenever you want.
